🏆 Team India's Cabinet of Glory

Milestone ICC Trophies that defined generations of Indian Cricket

1983

Prudential World Cup

Kapil Dev's underdog India shocked West Indies at Lord's to start a revolution.

2007

ICC T20 World Cup

MS Dhoni's young brigade defeated Pakistan in Johannesburg in the inaugural edition.

2011

ICC ODI World Cup

Ended a 28-year wait on home soil at Wankhede Stadium with Dhoni's iconic six.

2024

ICC T20 World Cup

Rohit Sharma led an unbeaten campaign in Barbados, ending India's ICC trophy drought.

Sachin Ramesh Tendulkar

The Master Blaster (1989 - 2013)

Unbreakable Records:

  • Most international runs in history: 34,357 Runs
  • Only cricketer with 100 International Centuries (51 Test + 49 ODI)
  • First player to score a double century in ODI cricket (200* vs SA in 2010)
  • Most matches played in Cricket History: 200 Tests & 463 ODIs
"I have seen God, he bats at no. 4 for India." – Matthew Hayden
×

Kapil Dev Nikhanj

Haryana Hurricane (1978 - 1994)

Unbreakable Records:

  • Only player in cricket history to combine 4,000+ Test runs and 400+ Test wickets.
  • Youngest captain to win an ODI World Cup (Age 24 in 1983).
  • Legendary 175* against Zimbabwe after India was down 17/5 in 1983 World Cup.
"When you have to make a choice and don't make it, that is in itself a choice."
×

Mahendra Singh Dhoni

Captain Cool (2004 - 2019)

Unbreakable Records:

  • Only captain to win T20 World Cup (2007), ODI World Cup (2011), and Champions Trophy (2013).
  • Fastest stumping record in international cricket: 0.08 seconds.
  • Most matches as captain in international cricket: 332 matches.
"Dhoni finishes off in style. India lift the World Cup after 28 years!" – Ravi Shastri
×

Virat Kohli

The King (2008 - Present)

Updated Career Records:

  • 86 International Centuries (55 ODI + 29 Test + 1 T20I + 1 Latest).
  • Crossed 15,000 ODI runs and 27,500+ total international runs.
  • Most runs in a single ODI World Cup edition: 765 runs (2023 World Cup).
"As long as Virat Kohli is at the crease, no chase is impossible."

Abhishek Sharma

Fearless Power-Hitter (2024 - Present)

Record-Breaking Milestone:

  • Smashed the fastest T20I century by an Indian (30 balls) vs Afghanistan, breaking Rohit Sharma's 35-ball record.
  • Fastest T20I hundred by any batter representing an ICC Full Member nation.
  • Scored 108 off 34 balls with 9 fours and 11 sixes at a strike rate of 317.65.
  • Highest individual score in the powerplay in men's T20Is (76* off 22 balls).
"I just hit the ball if it is in my zone, simple as that."
